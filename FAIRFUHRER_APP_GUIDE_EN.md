# FairFuhrer Mobile App
## A Clear Guide to Our App Concept

---

## Introduction: Why a Mobile App?

### The Problem

Imagine: A family from Munich is on vacation at Lake Constance. They're standing on a hiking trail in the mountains and want to find a sustainable restaurant for lunch. They open the FairFuhrer website on their phone, but...

❌ **No Internet** – there's often no reception in the mountains

❌ **Slow** – the website needs good internet

❌ **Inconvenient** – entering the URL every time

### The Solution

A native mobile app solves all these problems:

✅ **Works offline** – maps and info are stored on the phone

✅ **Fast** – opens in 1 second

✅ **Always at hand** – an icon on the home screen

**Conclusion:** The app isn't a replacement for the website, but an extension for on-the-go – exactly where tourists need it most.

## Part 1: How Do We Make Money?

### The Freemium Model Explained

**What does "Freemium" mean?**

The word combines "Free" and "Premium" (paid). The idea:

- The **basic version is free** → many people download the app
- **Extended features cost money** → some users pay for them

**Why does this work?**

Real-world example: The app "Komoot" (outdoor navigation) uses exactly this model. They have over 30 million users because the basic version is free. About 5% pay for Premium – but 5% of 30 million is 1.5 million paying customers.

## Part 2: What Do the Individual Features Mean?

### Offline Maps

**What is it?**
The user can download a region (e.g., "Lake Constance") to their phone. After that, the map works without internet.

**What does it look like in practice?**
1. User opens the app (with internet)
2. Clicks on "Download region"
3. Selects "Lake Constance" (approx. 45 MB)
4. Waits 1-2 minutes
5. Done! Now everything works offline

**Why is this important?**
- There's often no reception in the mountains
- Roaming abroad is expensive
- Battery lasts longer without constant data connection

**Who else does this?**
Google Maps, Komoot, Maps.me – all successful map apps offer offline mode.

---

### Audio Guides

**What is it?**
For each location, there's a voice recording (1-3 minutes) explaining the history and special features.

**What does it look like in practice?**
1. User stands in front of "Öko-Bauernhof Müller"
2. Opens the app, taps on the location
3. Presses "Play audio"
4. Hears: *"Öko-Bauernhof Müller was founded in 1952 and has been bio-certified since 2010. The Müller family places special emphasis on..."*

**Why is this important?**
- User doesn't have to read (practical while hiking)
- Feels like a personal guided tour
- Higher perceived value = people pay for it

**Who else does this?**
Museums, city apps (e.g., "Berlin Travel Guide"), Audible

---

### Favorites

**What is it?**
The user can "like" places and save them in a personal list.

**Free version:** Maximum 5 favorites
**Premium:** Unlimited

**Why the limitation?**
Psychological trick: When someone has 5 favorites and wants to save the 6th, they think: "I use the app so much – €7.99 is worth it."

---

### Geolocation

**What is it?**
The app knows where the user currently is and shows nearby places.

**What does it look like in practice?**
1. User opens the app
2. App asks: "May FairFuhrer use your location?"
3. User allows it
4. Map automatically centers on their location
5. Shows: "3 sustainable places nearby"

**Why is this important?**
Nobody wants to search manually. The app should immediately show: "This is near you!"

---

### Geofencing (Premium only)

**What is it?**
The app sends a notification when the user comes near an interesting place – even when the app is closed.

**What does it look like in practice?**
1. User walks through Lindau
2. Their phone vibrates
3. Notification: "📍 You're 200m from the Öko-Café! Open until 6:00 PM"
4. User thinks: "Oh, I'll check that out!"

**Why is this Premium?**
- Technically complex
- Uses some battery
- Real added value for active users

---

### Themed Routes

**What is it?**
Pre-planned tours with multiple stops, e.g., "Sustainable Day Trip at Lake Constance."

**What does it look like in practice?**

```
Route: "Eco-Gourmet Tour Allgäu"
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Stop 1: Organic bakery for breakfast (9:00 AM)
    ↓ 15 min drive
Stop 2: Nature park hike (10:00 AM)
    ↓ 20 min drive
Stop 3: Sustainable restaurant (1:00 PM)
    ↓ 10 min drive
Stop 4: Eco-farm visit (3:00 PM)
```

**Free version:** 1 sample route
**Premium:** All routes + create your own

**Why is this valuable?**
Tourists don't want to plan themselves. A ready-made route saves time and gives confidence: "Someone thought this through for me."

---

### AR View (Augmented Reality)

**What is it?**
The user holds up their phone (like taking a photo), and the app shows on the camera image where the places are.

**What does it look like in practice?**
1. User stands at a viewpoint
2. Opens the AR view
3. Holds up the phone
4. On the screen, they see the real landscape + markers:
   - "← Eco-Hotel (500m)"
   - "→ Bio-Restaurant (300m)"

**Why is this cool?**
- "Wow effect" – impresses users
- Practical in unfamiliar surroundings
- Differentiates us from other apps

---

### "Open Now" Filter

**What is it?**
A button that only shows places that are currently open.

**What does it look like in practice?**
1. It's 7:00 PM
2. User is looking for a restaurant
3. Activates "Show only open"
4. App filters out all closed places
5. Bonus: "⚠️ Öko-Café closes in 30 min"

**Why is this important?**
Nothing is more frustrating than driving to a place that's closed.

---

### Smart Suggestions (Weather-based)

**What is it?**
The app suggests places that match the current weather.

**What does it look like in practice?**

**When it rains:**

**When it's sunny:**

```
• Nature trail (3 km)
```

**Why is this valuable?**
The app "thinks along" – that feels personal and helpful.

## Part 4: How Do Partners Manage Their Content?

### The Role System Explained

We use Directus (our existing content management system) with different permission levels:

---

#### 👑 Admin (Us – Seenergien)

**What can we do?**
- See and edit everything
- Create new partners
- Approve or reject partner content
- View statistics for everyone

---

#### ✏️ Editor (Our editors)

**What can they do?**
- Edit all places
- Add new places
- Review and approve partner content

**Why do we need this?**
As we grow, we can hire editors to maintain content.

---

#### 🏢 Partner

**What can they do?**
- Edit ONLY their own places
- Upload photos (max. 10)
- Write text (max. 2000 characters)
- Upload audio (max. 3 minutes)

**What can they NOT do?**
- See or edit other places
- Publish content directly (must be approved by us)

**Why the restrictions?**

| Rule | Reason |
|------|--------|
| Max. 10 photos | Storage costs money |
| Max. 2000 characters | Consistent quality, users won't read more |
| Max. 3 min audio | File size, consistency |
| Approval required | We check quality, prevent spam/errors |

---

#### 👁️ Viewer (optional)

**What can they do?**
- Only read, can't edit
- View statistics

**Why do we need this?**
For investors, advisors, or analysts who need insight but shouldn't change anything.

---

### The Process: From Partner to App

```
1. Partner logs in
   → Sees only their own profile

2. Partner changes something
   → "New opening hours: Mon-Fri 9 AM - 6 PM"
   → Clicks "Save"

3. Status: "Waiting for approval"
   → Change is NOT live immediately

4. Our editor reviews
   → Is everything correct?
   → No spelling errors?
   → Photos in good quality?

5. Editor approves
   → Status: "Published"
   → Change is now visible in the app

(If there are problems)
5b. Editor rejects
    → Partner receives message: "Please correct..."
    → Partner revises
    → Back to step 3
```
